What we keep

  • Stored: your name and email, your password as a one-way hash (nobody can read it), your AI chats (your questions and the replies), a usage record for each reply (when, which chat, how many tokens), your direct messages, and when you were last online.
  • Who can see it: the text of an AI chat is sent to the AI provider to write the reply, and administrators can open AI chats and the usage log. A direct message is visible only to the two people in the conversation.
  • How long: until you delete your account. Deleting removes all of the above, including your direct conversations, which disappear for the other person too.
